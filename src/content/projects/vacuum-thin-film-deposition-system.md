## Project Overview

At Nanohmics VAC Lab, supported scientists and engineers with thin-film deposition, prototype fixture assembly, and cleanroom vacuum technology.

### Key Technical Contributions
- **High-Vacuum Operations**: Operated and maintained turbomolecular and closed-loop cryopump systems achieving base pressures $< 1.0 \times 10^{-7}\text{ mbar}$.
- **Deposition Monitoring**: Monitored real-time deposition rates and total film thickness using Quartz Crystal Microbalance (QCM) controllers.
- **Vacuum Integrity**: Conducted fine helium mass spectrometer leak detection (DIN EN 1779 / ISO 20485) and plasma cleaning of silicon/glass substrates.
