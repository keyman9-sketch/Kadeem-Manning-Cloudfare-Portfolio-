## Project Overview

With the Peace Corps in Gaborone, Botswana, performed instrumentation, controls, and automation loop implementation for gray-water facilities and community infrastructure.

### Key Technical Contributions
- **PLC Programming & Commissioning**: Configured and programmed Siemens S7-1200/S7-1500 (TIA Portal) and AutomationDirect Productivity Series controllers.
- **Advanced Sensing Loops**: Integrated inline oil-condition sensors, distributed acoustic sensing (DAS), and ultrasonic flow meters into central SCADA monitoring systems.
